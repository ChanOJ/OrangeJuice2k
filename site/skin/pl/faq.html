Q> What is OJ's update frequency ?
A> There is no predefined timing. As soon as there is something new or to change, we change it, and we translate it (note: with a little delay ;-).

Q> Why are some images compressed in the gfx compo's reports ?
A> When you click on the image preview, the original image made by the graphist appears. Because the graphist draws the picture pixel by pixel, it has to be kept so, and has not to be modified ! (we switch directly from LBM, IFF or PCX to GIF, without any retouch). In the case of a 24 bytes image, the JPG compression is tested, if changes are visible, the gfx is compressed in a RAR file. That way, downloadable gfx compos on Orange Juice are of equal quality as on FTP or BBS.

Now the PiNG format is almost perfectly supported by the browsers, so I will use it in the future.

Q> What's all this then, with these RAR archives, why not simply use ZIP ?
A> Evolution is required, else why not use windows 3.1 (sorry for those who do) . WINRAR is much more efficient than WINZIP. With it's resizable dictionary (like the dos fat) and it's good compression rate, RAR compresses a 2MB text file to 160 kB, whereas ZIP only makes it to 400 kB, with an equal run time. That way, connection time is saved, and our web site's host transmission band is less required.

If the reason is just you forgot your password, then simply enter a bad password and then follow the instructions.
