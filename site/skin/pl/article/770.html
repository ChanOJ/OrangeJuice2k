Interview with Bstates [04 Jun 2000]

Lassi aka Bstates versus Chris aka Yes :)

Yes|OJ> Hi lassi, you was the first to make a live session on nectarine radio, where did you take this idea ?

Bstates> oh yes, its just something which has been bothering me for quite a long time, i mean, the concept of broadcasting something live on the net, and nectarine was there so that fit our needs perfectly.

Yes|OJ> Can you tell us who participated to the live ?

Bstates> i can! me, antti koivisto, tuomas munck, tuomo korhonen, esa ruoho and antti jädertpolm. esa and antti were in technical support department administrating the broadcasting machine :)

Yes|OJ> which kind of hardware did you use for your live ?

Bstates> first of all we had the very secret brothomstates studio core system, two turntables, a bunch of effects and drum machines.. everything connected together in a perverse way which allowed us to do practically anything to the sound.
All human controlled of course :)

Yes|OJ> of course :)

Bstates> but i must remind you that none of the stuff we played was pre-sequenced or anything. It all was as you heard it.

Yes|OJ> i trust you :)

Bstates> which i like, cos its not something you could predict. you'll never know what happens next and thats one part of the deal..

Bstates> ive been asked that also, if someone wants to record all the live sessions we might be doing, its all right for me as long as its 56kbps :)

Yes|OJ> i know that md did it :)

Bstates> yeah he did it, but he only has two first hours of it on minidisc.
We could have recorded it ourselves but i think we were a wee bit busy already anyway :P

Yes|OJ> hehe i understand ;) Ok so if someone recorded last 3 hours, then he must contact us :)

Bstates> if he wants to.

Bstates> and yet i want this thing to stay unique in a way. An experience you cant just play back :)

Yes|OJ> hehe :) Any chance to hear a live from you at LTP ? :)

Bstates> well it'd be nice to play something at LTP but i think its a bit too much gear to take to france :)

Yes|OJ> gosh you're right :) try to rent a bus ;)

Bstates> grand !

Yes|OJ> ok thanks lassi to gime some of your time, want to do last word ? :)

Bstates> keep it real, keep it irish (courtesy of thomas puha)
First of all i want to thank you and chandra, plus everyone else involved in this.

Ps : Nectarine must thanks Bstates and all live players :) for their work, and you must know that Ojuice bandwith has been fully employed during their live sesion with 52 listeners at peak :)
