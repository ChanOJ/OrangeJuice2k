V2OS [13 Dec 1999]
V2OS is a brand new 32 bit OS for PC. Full written in asm, it's built for speed and is compact. Altough it actually lacks some vital features like an usable shell, drivers and utilities, and is monotasked (Damn ! Are we in 1989 ? :) But this will probably change), i think some talented coders should give it a try. This OS is in earlier development stages, and is a healthy basis for what could be a great alternative OS.
