Banners exchange system

Our banners exchange system features weekly and monthly reports ,views, clicks, click rate, expiration date, priority, border or not, target window and alt tag.

The banners may be of one or both of the two following formats :
- 420 pixels width (60 pixels height max, 15kB max)
- 170 pixels width (30 pixels height max, 5kB max)

I've no time for the moment to code an ASP to make the procedure more easy, but send your banner(s) to our email.
Don't forget to tell us your nick id (you can see it by watching the URL to your OJ handle sheet); if you don't find it tell us your nick/groups.
You can also tell us a begin date and/or an end date (usefull for a party banner) to display the banner, if you prefer to have a 1 pixel border around the banner, the target window (the default is _blank) and the content of the alt tag (displayed during the load of the banner).

You don't need to have submitted a banner to display the banners in your website. You just have to add this code (in one line) where you want the banner to be inserted:

<SCRIPT language="Javascript" src="http://www.ojuice.net/banner420.js"></SCRIPT>

(replace 420 by 170 to insert a 170px width banner)

You can see an example of banner insertion on the upper right corner of the main page of Orange Juice.

You can also force the border, the target or the text of the banner. For example, replace src="http://www.ojuice.net/banner420.js"

- by src="http://www.ojuice.net/banner420/noborder.js"
(to have no border around the banner)

- by src="http://www.ojuice.net/banner420/noborder/self.js"
(to display the site linked by the banner in the current window and to have no border around the banner)

- by src="http://www.ojuice.net/banner420/border/blank.js"
(to have a border around the banner and to display the site linked in a new window)

- by src="http://www.ojuice.net/banner420/border/notxt.js"
(to have a border around the banner and a no text below the banner)

- by src="http://www.ojuice.net/banner420/alttxt.js"
(to have the alt text displayed below the banner)

- by ...
