OGLChallenge [05 Sep 2000]



----


The OpenGL Challenge
presents this week's Free Style entries. Many of this week's challenge entries are interactive, with popup menus or keys that control their behavior. Dazzle yourself with a program similar to Maya painter called Butterfly Paint.
Butterflies wander across the screen, painting trails with the dust from their wings! Have a look at Aniso Light which demonstrates lighting similar to the kind found when looking at etched steel. If those lights don't make your jaw drop, wait until you see Sparx! It takes particle systems to another levels with Volumetric Lighting and Procedural Textures! Play around with A 3D Case Study Using OpenGL by Fotis which is the culmination of his work while developing an OpenGL e-book for the Developer's Gallery.
Have a Mac? Fountain, created by Calumr, displays a simple fountain effect with a console system that allows full control over colour, number of particles, gravity, rate of time flow, and key bindings. Finally, if you have linux and are itching to try out some 3D accelerated graphics, have a look at Torus by Sjonny. Torus also runs on Windows. Interested in joining the OpenGL Challenge? This week's theme is Rockets.
Imagine the effects that a rocket could generate on launch, flight, and impact.
