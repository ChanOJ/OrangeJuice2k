A little bit bored ;) [11 Oct 2000]

Some people are saying we at OJ are ripping a little bit more than we may. In the demoscene, "to rip" is a very pejorative word so I had to answer that. To these people I'll say read and take care about the following and shut the fuck up for ever.

The only things we've ripped are :

1) "the last demos" on the right, ripped from demoscene.org.
It was an "eye for eye" action (a money pay back if you prefer ;). for your information it'll no more exist in this form in OJv3. An already existing website will take the relay.

2) "Nectarine", ripped from the french non-demoscene radio "Zone 51".
Can we say that ABC have ripped the idea to CNN to make a video channel ? Same thing for the radios... I remember that "to rip" in the demoscene context is a pejorative word.

3) the "banners exchange" ripped from the party ban ex (when it died).
I've added the banners exchange on OJ when I saw that The Party one did'nt worked anymore. So I cannot say that it's a rip...

4) the piclib, ripped from www.m4de.com (website in which I've participed),
but extended to the demoscene and automated. the photo idea coming from slengpung.
I've to say that we at OJ have *very* good relations with slengpung, so talk about what you know and stop play to be scandalmonger. Btw, hi Arcane, some other french song learned ? ;)


What we *do not* have ripped :

- the oneliner. it's on OJ for more than 2 years, and the idea comes from the BBSs (OJ's first version was a BBS one) and CITEWEB (a french free web hosting service *made* by Tonic, Gizmo and I). We've have noted that for now a lot of websites have added a oneliner (named differently, because all lot of webmasters don't know why it's calld "oneliner", just have a look to the BBSs). And I've received *a lot* of emails asking me to send the script.
We never complain about that, on the contrary I'm very glad to see this idea everywhere.

- the ftp search. the idea comes from hornet.org, but hornet.org isn't a ftp search but a single search on its own archive.

- the forums. Two years ago there were a forum on OJ (when it was hosted by citeweb). For a long it's no more active, but it'll revive in OJv3, and it'll not be a rip. Don't talk of rip about things that do not yet exist, lamer.

What have been ripped from OJ :

- the countries flags. They've be done by Zone / Psykotrope especially for Orange Juice, re-used on CITEWEB (I was one the webmaster and I've asked the permission to Zone). These flags are on a lot of websites (some I've received the autorisation of I and Zone) on the web for now, even *commercials* websites like voila.fr (in the ftp search).
*Thanks* to use the work of free-working people for commercial use.

- the oneliner, but once again I'm glad of it.

- the hudge automated people database. I'm not specially gald of it because I don't see the interrest to do *exactly* the same thing elsewhere. I remember that Orange Juice at his begining (3 years ago) was *only* this people database, nothing else.


As a conclusion I'll say that Orange Juice is sometimes getting some ideas with the goal to make them better. We're are not ripping for ripping (except for the "last demos", but once again it was an eye for eye ;).
