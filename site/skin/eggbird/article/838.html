TakeOver 2000 - realtime report [17 Jun 2000]

Hey Guys, so we are at Takeover 2000 and as usual we will try to make a small report in realtime.

06/16/2000 - 9:00pm

The french meeting point was my flat and just after skal arrived, we were waiting for Thor / Tpolm coming from Made home. We left paris at 9.30pm with our second Ojmobil Car. Nice travel with some 70' songs ;). Just only one problem : Like all french people i think we lost maybe one hour in belgium, looking for road names. It's amazing, you have pannels with E19-E42 and on the road you have another A19 and other indications everywhere. So only one idea : GOING Outside from Belgium and FAST !! where is the north :) ? So after 5 hours we arrived at party place. Taking some guilders just before. It was 3.30am and silent room was very quiet.

~~ SLEEP BREAK ~~
Notice : Takeovers organization is maybe too hard. Like for TP or other parties, you can't sleep inside. And they come again and gain to see if you don't sleep. Ok so let's go sleeping outside, FORBIDDEN TOO, you can sleep here, it's forbidden, go to the sleeping room or in your car ! Maybe thos guys must notice that : Sleeping room is far from here and last years lot of dutch peoples were screaming during the night and maybe they must notice too that when you are 4 in a car it's very hard to sleep inside !!!
~~ SLEEP BREAK ~~

06/17/2000 - 8.30am : Sleeping is stupid when you must meet lot of scene peoples so i decided to wakeup. First surprise, noone in silent room is sleeping. Second surprise DAP himself is coming to say (very loud indeed) that SILENT ROOM IS A SILENT ROOM AND WE CAN'T MAKE ANY NOISE !!!
Gosh : We (french people) asked FOR THIS silent room last year but there is a difference between a hall where GAMERZ had 10000 Watts soundsystem and us who just try demo or something else and not with LOUD sound. STOP PLEASE, it's not a jail HERE !!!! Are you crazy ? You sell Beer inside (even for drunk people) you authorize smoking and we can't sleep inside and we can't make a little bit of noise ? hmmmm :(

06/17/2000 - 9:15am
Skal is the first from my car to try to eat Dutch Food. He is green now ;)
Eating cheese and sausages at 9 am is very strange for us :))))

End of the first report, Stay tuned :o)
