DJ isabelle Answer - The end [17 May 2000]

Answer from DJ ISABELLE on a lot of mail....

Dear music makers,

I saw a lot of messages about me and a track that was post on my site called
"Eternal Flame".

This is the short story:

Two month's ago I was contacted by mail from a boy who called himself
DJ(...). He send me a unfinished (sic) track that he called ETERNAL FLAME (a
fastracker file) and told me that he want make finished the track toghether.
I was very impressed from what I hear, and told that to him.

He asked me "finished the track". In my opinion the track was more than
finished so I made only some personal changes on it and make a few extra's
on it.

So after a lot of mail in both ways I sent him the final track that I made
from it.

2 weeks later he asked me to put the track on my website, because only 40
people visits his own site and asked me to publish it under my name because
he wants to now what paople are thinking about it.

I did that, just to helping a guy from who I was thinking he was a honnest
young DJ that was looking for some attention. That was the reflection of the
person that I had from the one with who I e-mailed.

Just 8 hours after I post the track on my site I received a e-mail from a
boy who called Martijn Jacobs and told me that HE is the real maker of that
track. The same day I received a lot of mail from a company called RBI MUSIC
that told me the owner of that tune.

On that moment I realised me that I was f*cked by a frustrated man/woman who
used my mother-instinct to get some attention.

That same day I removed the track IMMEDIANTLY from my website and send a lot
of mail with Martijn and his RBI MUSIC-friends with an exploination of the
story and my EXCUSES for the whole strange story.

I asked Martijn also to his phone number to have a friendly talk with him to
explain the whole story from woman to man....

I still wait on it....

BTW: This story is more then hard to me because I make not a secret of my
samples that I use in my own tracks and also in my remixes.

(PS: I have still all the e-mails from DJ(...) to me and back. For the
records.

Greetings,

DJ Isabelle
