What ?
Nectarine is the first demoscene radio broadcasting every days some of the best songs done by sceners since 1988. Almost all the musics are free of right but belong to their respective authors. You are not authorized to record sessions but you can come into contact with their authors by our intermediary (if we have their emails) or you can try to reach them using the Orange Juice Database.

When ?
Nectarine (OJRADIO) is on air every days 24/24 7/7.

How to listen ?
Click here

How do you stream ?
We have a 8Mb/s ATM line - We stream directly from SAM to Winamp and then to a shoutcast server (actually 1.666.1) under Windows NT2000 and relayed with live365.com server. The Mods are played with modplug then converted one by one to mp3 format. Our server broadcasts at 24kbps-22khz (due to the limit of the RTC users) and 56kbps-22khz and we can accept more than 744 listeners with all relay server.

Where did you get all those tunes ?
The Atari songs are taken from DMA / Sector One playlist, the MODs come from MODS ANTHOLOGY by Gryzor, the SID come from HVC and Unlock / Vantage, the Amiga tunes are taken from Exotica. MP3 are from authors like Vic, Wayfinder etc.. who send us their own mp3, and we promote CD from those Label : Audiophonik, Aural Planet, Level-D and RBI.

Can I broadcast my own music ?
Yes of course, we have opened a FTP server just for you (ftp.ojuice.net | user : ojradio | pass : ojradio) where you can upload all the songs (MP3 format very recommanded !!) you want to broadcast. Of course it's a "write only" server so your songs are protected, nobody (except us ;) can download 'em. Don't forget to create a dir with your handle and to upload a small infofile where you give us all the info we need and a small text giving us the right to broadcast your music.

I want advertise my partie, cd, compo, website
You are a partie organizer ? a webmaster of a demoscene website ? a musician ? you want to advertise for your work ? No problem, just send on our FTP the advert (mp3 format) and we will broadcast your advert.
