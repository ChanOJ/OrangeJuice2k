New(ish) program: module indexer
23 Sep 2000

I wrote a little util that searches through your computer and lists your music in a nice and neat report, with title, filename, and size. One warning, it's really huge... I'd say 44kb is certainly way too much :)
Features: sorting by title or filename, HTML generator (with hyperlinks!), list caching, and a bit more stuff.
Filetypes: IT, S3M, XM, MOD, MTM, FAR, 669, MT2, STM, WOW, and LIQ, as well as kind-of support for MP3 tags (disabled by default because it isn't quite perfect yet). You can define custom filetypes as well, by specifying byte offset and length of the title.
