Scene Event 2000 - What's happening ?


A summary.

In 1999 Summer Encounter was relocated to Aalborg. A lot
of us who took part in the creation of Summer Encounter
back in '95 feel that this relocation was a step in the
wrong direction. We feel that we forgot our roots and lost
the "Summer Encounter-atmosphere" as a result.

After the Summer Encounter general meeting in March 2000,
there was some uncertainty as to whether the Summer
Encounter association would be dissolved. Due to this
situation we (SWAMP and DiSTORTiON) decided to carry on
the festival tradition as in "the old days", creating a
new association called SE2K.

Taking for granted that the festival would go on as usual,
we decided not to involve the guests in these internal
matters. In the meantime it has been established that the
Summer Encounter association as such is not to be disolved.
As a result of this developement we (SE2K) cannot call our
festival "Summer Encounter" because this name is the trade-
mark of the Summer Encounter association. We have therefore
chosen to name our festival "Scene Event 2000" (or SE2K if
you will).



Scene Event - Back To Basics.

SE2K wishes to recreate the atmosphere we remember from the
days when Summer Encounter took place in Harridslev. That's
why we're going "back to basics" and hosting our festival in
Harridslev. In our oppinion it's the ideal location. It's
important to us that our guest have the opportunity to meet
face to face so they can socialize and have a good time. It's
not enough to have a place to put your computer - There has
to be atmosphere ! Thats why we prioritize social aspects
such as the barbequeparty and the tentcity highly. More on
these matters on our website : http://se2k.dk



Rumors, propaganda and other nonsense.


Throughout the last few months a lot of rumors have been
flourishing as to what SE2K is really all about. There are
a few "rumor-starters" out there who are very keen to damage
us and persuade people not to attend our festival. One of
these rumors state that "SE2K is a pure game-party". We'd
like to kill this rumor once and for all : SE2K is first and
foremost a SCENE-PARTY. Hence the name Scene Event 2000. If
anyone should still be in doubt, consider this : Sceners have
access to seatreservation 2 weeks before everyone else !

Our purpose is to encourage creative use of computers. Games
and gamecompos are not the purposes of this festival. We will
however not forbid people to play games. It is our intention
to assemble as many scenepeople as possible from around the
globe. Sceners are our first priority. Seats not occupied by
sceners will be available to anyone interested in computers.
Let's not forget that future sceners have to come from some-
where. We where all kids once.

You can see a preview of our official poster here :
http://se2k.dk/poster/preview.jpg




Questions, anyone ?

To make it easier for everyone to have their questions about
SE2K answered, we have a mailinglist where you can discuss
the festival with organizers and other guests. To join the
list send a mail to :
list-request@se2k.dk with "subscribe your_email_address"
in the BODY field. Remember, you have to use the same address
as the one your're writing from - otherwise the mail will
bounce. If you have trouble or doubts with this, mail to :
sos@se2k.dk Please note that the mailinglinst is in english
only so everyone can understand what's being said.




Compo winners from Summer Encounter '99

We're quite unhappy with the fact that compo winners from
Summer Encounter '99 didn't get the prizes they deserved. As
we ourselves have been a part of Summer Encounter, we feel
that it is our duty to see that the winners from '99 get a
prize. We will attempt to supply prizes for these people at
SE2k. We cannot promise you cash though.




If you need to contact us, feel free to send us a mail at
party@se2k.dk



We hope to see you at Scene Event 2000


Best regards
