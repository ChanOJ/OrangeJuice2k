Yes and Chandra, interviewed by, ehm, OJ ;)

OJ> Hello, can you introduce yourself please.

Y> My name is Christophe Le Sage, I am 27 years old. My nickname is Yes^Orange Juice and I work as webmaster on this cool website :)

C> Glop, my name is Charles Sauthier, I'm 21, oops, 22, sorry, and I'm working in computers for 4 years (finished my studies 6 months ago, so working full-time for 6 months).

OJ> What is the scene ?

Y> Hum hard to explain but for me it's a passion. Scene is a door to meet cool people.

C> Gosh! The demoscene is imho a virtual gathering of people sharing the same idea of what a demo should be. Gna, happy ? ;)

OJ> How many years ago and how did you discover the scene ?

Y> It was in 1987. At this time I was on C64 (sorry it was for games ;o)) just two months before buying my first Atari ST (520 STF). The first demo I saw was a Rob Hubbard musikdisk.
One month later a good friend of mine called NCS (who was coder for IRIS and Moby's cousin) show me his Amiga 500 and I felt in love with this computer. I bought a brand new one one week after :)
But I was in the Oleron Island and meeting other people was very hard so I used to buy demos with a DP service called Bytman-The compilation and with other ones. All demos were in my hands 6 months after they was released but I was a big demo addict. My first shock was a small meeting I organized in 1992. It was the SSD party (Sea, Sun and Demo Party). Only 15 guys :) but Moby/DRD, Alan/Impact and the Austrian group Energy were here. (Hey fred tu te souviens de la baston de craie et de brosse à tableau :))

C> I got a computer late (I guess I was 12), in fact it was my father's. He bought it (an IBM PS/1) to do some financial work (Excel rulez =). I started to play games with it, then I get bored of playing and I discovered basica (the IBM basic). It was my first steps in programming. My cousin got an Atari 520, then an Amiga 500. He show me some demos and I felt in love with them. I've looked for PC demos for long time (no net, no modem, no friend in the PC underground world...) I finally managed to get some (via a friend of my cousin). And it was the revelation; EMF, Future Crew, waooo!
Later, in 1996, a colleague (thanks Ben ! :) invited me to go with him to the Saturne Party in France, 5 km from my home. The demos entries were Contrast/Oxygene, Bomb/Impact and Chapi-Chapo/Aegis. The intro entry I remember was Teddybear's Revenge/Eclipse (get the first place).
At the same time a friend at university (Tonic/Tnt, I'm working with him now) convinced me to buy a modem. I did so and I began to go on Dune BBS (the first big demo oriented french BBS), Fury (the BBS of my university friend) and later, A.C.E.

OJ> When and how was Orange Juice created ?

Y> I wasn't in the first team :) so it's hard to answer but I guess that Tonic, Gizmo and Chandra created it near 1997, if I have a good memory. First version was on Mygale.org. Later they created Citeweb.net (a free Hosting server). Orange Juice and Automag were the first websites on it. Now Orange Juice is on its own server.

C> The sysop of Dune BBS was Gizmo / Tiny Toons (I'm working with now too =), but Dune was created by Bisounours / Aegis^Fire^TnT^... (well known Ansi artist). Biz had the idea to make a big nicknames list to avoid people from getting an already taken nick. I agreed with him and began to make a big list by getting the nicknames on the parties results files :). Later we (Bisounours, Tonic and I) began to work on an html version of what will became Orange Juice.
Nowadays Orange Juice is hosted on my work machine (stop doing requests on the search engine when I'm working !! =) and Planet-d offers us the DNS.

OJ> What is your job in Orange Juice ?

Y> Hummm my first function was to make Orange Juice known. Chandra is a very good web programmer with a lot of very good ideas but at this time he was not very known in demoscene. So my first job was to put Orange Juice everywhere :))
Now I find news, I check them, make some bugs huntings, some interviews. I try to help Chandra :o)
I organize demo for Orange Juice and of course I'm main organizer of the LTP party who is now a 100% Orange Juice production since O.J. is a real french Loi 1901 (non profit) association.

C> Since the begining, I'm the main webmaster (Bisounours and Tonic stopped really soon to work on it). Since Orange Juice is dynamic I'm also the ASP-coder.

OJ> How many people work for the demo division of Orange Juice ?
Y> Hum hard to count them because I work only with good friends of mine and all come from other groups.
Orange Juice demo production is a melting pot of friendship guys. Only 2 weeks for Past and 1 for Amigold (Hey Ecstasy and Cyg : I LOVE YOU).

OJ> How many people work in Orange Juice ?
C> For the moment we are three. Chandra (me :), Yes (my neighbour in this interview ;), adding some news and new ideas) and Aevoc (managing the Links part)

OJ> How did you met Chandra ? :o)
Y> I have no memory :) It's very strange but for me I know Chandra since I come to Paris, in 1996. I think it was during Saturne Party 4 in April 96. At same time I meet in real Gryzor, Made, and some others cools guys like Snes and his Playmobil group :).

OJ> How did you met Yes ? :o)
C> I heard about him on the BBSs after the LTP1, I guess we virtually met on IRC, and at the Saturne 4 (also known as Saturne 96b) we really met and he offered me a video tape done at the LTP.

OJ> What is the thing you prefer in Chandra ?
Y> His talent. This guys KICK ASS. You give him a short deadline and you have what you asked him. It's a very good friend. When I was depressed it was the only one to cry with me ;o) ! This guys RULESSSSSSSSSSSS.

OJ> What is the thing you prefer in Yes ?
C> He is really full of kindness and absolutely in love with the scene. I'd never saw somebody as enthusiast about the scene as him. And when he wants to do something, even if everybody let him alone, he continues, and he succeeds in it.
And he's also a damn good friend, I love him :)

OJ> What does "LTP" mean? Where did you get the idea of such name for your Party ?
Y> Hahaha good question. LTP means 'Lucky and Tigrou Party". Lucky is my black cat (he did Wired 96 !!). Tigrou is the cat of Kenet. He was one of the LTP 1 organisers when i was in .rIBBON!

OJ> What does "Orange Juice" mean? Where did you get the idea of such name for your website ?
C> The name "Orange Juice" is due to Bisounours. When I asked him the question he said me something like "It's better than Apple Juice".

OJ> Yes: in 1997 you decided to create LTP. How did you have this idea ?
Y> During the Wired 96. I met Poï, Moaï and Kenet. I remember our first conversation. Their monitor was black and they checked everywhere what was wrong. My first sentence was 'hey guy, why don't you put the contrast ON :o))". At the end of the party I joined them in .rIBBON! and my first think was to organize a SMALL meeting only with 10 friends. At the end the LTP was a cool party with 100 REAL demosceners and no gamers in a VERY small room. Almost all was perfect (the room was toooo smallll).

OJ> Chandra: in 1999 you decided to join the LTP Main-Org team. How did you have this idea ?
C> I'm not enough perseverant to be a good demo coder, and I've no gift to be a graphician or a musician. But I love the demos, so I became a scene webmaster, and as it's wasn't enough I had the idea to become a party organizer.
A demo, a gfx or a music is a palpable thing, a website or a party isn't. I love people doing their best to make a demo better than the preceding one, but I don't like people making a website or a party just to make better than an existing one. I prefer collaborate in an existing one to make it become better.
That's why I offered my services to Dr.Yes (he got this nickname at this time).

OJ> Yes: how many people helped you for LTP ?
Y> Hum we were 6 at LTP 1 for 100 peoples :), during LTP 2 we were more (12) for 250 peoples. We were 14 during LTP 3. 15 with the security agent :).

OJ> Chandra: were you at LTP 1 and 2 ?
C> I wasn't at the LTP1 because it was a private party, it means the invitations were distributed on some BBSs. One of these BBSs was A.C.E, but due to a lack of money I'd stopped to connect to it.
I was at the LTP2, I offered my services to make the entry and vote systems but not as an organizer. After that I decided to be a real organizer of the LTP3.

OJ> What is the difference(s) between the LTPs and the other parties

Y> As you know, LTP is my child. I put all my passion in it and my passion is DEMO. I hate playing lot of time (ok sometimes I play like everyone but not all my time :)))) so I made LTP only for Sceners and specially for old sceners who loves meeting people during party without any computer :). So LTP is a real demoscene event without any gamers (the last one was blinded during the LTP3 ;o)).

C> Our goal isn't to get as many people as possible, but to get as many scener as possible. Another goal isn't to offer the biggest prices for the compos, but to offer the most fun. I guess some parties have these goals, but unfortunately not the biggest ones.

OJ> What did you plan new for the LTP4 ?

Y> A powerfull laser to burn gamers :o)))). My first idea is to keep our spirit. I think LTP3 wasn't perfect so I need to work a lot of time to organize something COOL.
Want a preview ? Perhaps will you see some old sceners playing in real but it's a secret.

C> We planned to do what we haven't the time to do at the LTP3. You'll see :)

OJ> What is your best scene memory ?

Y> Shodan's and other people's 'Hurraaa' at the end of LTP2. I'm very sensible so I cried :)
And of course the LTP 2 and 3 end photo with ALL peoples. Nice to see Bstate, aka Dune/Orange, making the Human-Wave :o)) and meeting Zodiak / Cascada at LTP party was a big shock :)

C> The Saturne Party 3, my first party. I was considering the demomakers as Gods and when I saw Contrast on the big screen, it was ... great ! Now I'm a little bit less naive, and a demo is not as magic for me as it was :(

OJ> Is there things you hate or don't appreciate in the scene nowadays?
Y> Joker, next question please.
C> The money prices and the gamers. I think a demomaker should make a demo for the fun, not for money. And I think a demoparty should gather sceners, not gamers.

OJ> What are you doing else out of your scene life ?

Y> Now I'm the Technical Director in a company. I work as a network administrator for 50 small compagnies and like consultant for 200 others :)

C> I'm working in computers, Internet. I'm programming dynamic websites (ASP and Visual C). I think Windows NT is a great OS and IIS is a great webserver, better than Apache. I don't understand people blocking on "Windows sucks and Linux rulez".
If you don't want your webserver to hang, just learn to make clean code :)

OJ> Yes : will you continue to organise LTP in the future ?
Y> I don't know. As every years I say "it's my last one" but as every years people ask for it so I continue :)

OJ> Chandra: will you continue to update Orange Juice in the future ?
C> As long as I got friends in the scene and get fun to met them.

OJ> Did you meet some demomakers out of the scene events ?

Y> Yes of course, Skal/bomb, Made/bomb, Traven/syndrome, Boz/bomb and Gandalf/planet-d. It's a good team :) and Chandra of course.

C> Yes, Yes (argh, what a nick, Yes, difficult to make understandable sentences talking about Yes =) for example, but also Made, Traven, Skal, Gandalf, Boz, Malon, Z, Kacy, ......

OJ> If someone wanted to make friends with you, what would be the worst and best words ?

Y> Best one will be : Hello, could we speak :)
Worst one will be : Can we play at Quake ? :)

C> The bests would be "Who are you ?" and the worsts would be "Gosh, you're Chandra ! wooo !" (fortunately the second case never occurs :).

OJ> What are your plans for the future ?

Y> Hahaha good question. Now my computer is off lot of time. I realised that my PC isn't my life :) Now I earn much more money so I will go to USA, Japan, and Canada this year, all is fine :)

C> Making Orange Juice getting better (the full text search is arriving, I just need to upgrade my work machine to a dual Pentium III 500 with a little bit more RAM :), having fun with my scene and non-scene friends.

OJ> What do you want to say to the world ?

Y> I hope you love our work ! :)

C> Stop to destroy the Earth, stop killing people, stop hurting people, even stop *annoying* people coz this is the best way to the end of the violence. Don't make to the others what you don't want they make to you. It's evident, but it doesn't seem so :(

OJ> What is the thing you did not have the time or anything to do yet and you absolutely want to do one day ?

Y> Buying my own house in front of the sea in south-west of France.

C> Visit the Iceland, coz this is the perfect cohabitation of the hostile Nature and the Human being (and it's also the country of Björk =)

OJ> If you were not French, from which country would you like to be ?
Y> Only one answer, CANADA !!!
C> I don't know, I don't like questions begining with "If".

OJ> Who won the last World Championship Soccer Contest ?
Y> Hahahahaha I hate soccer but I know it's France :)
C> I don't really like soccer, but that's Us, the Froggies, Yeah ! :)

OJ> Say the first thing which goes through your mind.
Y> Demoscene is what you do with it.
C> glou ((c) KacyGLOU :)

OJ> And now it's greetings time, let's go dudes !

Y> Lot of guys but first ones will be Skal and Chandra. Just after I will say hello to Made, Traven, Gandalf, Nytrik, Towerx, Statix, Md, Ior, Bstate, Virago, Ps, Shodan, Rez, Malon, Nogsf, Lisa and for end I will send a big kiss to Valérie, Bélinda, Isabelle, Vanessa, Carole, Olive, Eric, David, Fanny, Nath, Agnès and all other I forgot.

C> Statix coz it's the best demo coder (design and power of code) I ever seen, Skal coz he comes just after Statix (very close to him :), Made coz even he makes especially copies, he's the best pixel drawer I've ever seen, Traven coz he's the best musician of the scene, Clawz and Moby coz their musics made me doing some beautiful dreams (Mobyle rulezz:), EMF, Future Crew and Triton coz they made imho the best megademos on PC at the start of the PC-scene life, when everybody was saying "PC sucks". And Yes, coz he has organized 3 LTPs and he's organizing a fourth one.
