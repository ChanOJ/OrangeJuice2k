What is a demo ?

A demo is program (sure ;o)) ! Like videoclips but in realtime. Done by coders, graphists and musik composers, those programs are free and done only to prove that they can do impossible things with a computer.

When demos are born ?

Demos are born in 80's with C64 computer done by Commodore. But the best time was with Amiga 500 and Atari ST computers. Now you can find demos on Amiga (500, 1200 and >), Atari (520, 1040 and >), Archimede, SGI Station, Dec Alpha and of course on Pc station (Dos, windows, linux). In this website you can only find windows demos done in D3D, Opengl or Glide.

What is a demomaker ?

A demomaker is a man or a woman who works for fun and not for money. It's normaly a young people. Normaly they done groups to work together.

Where can we find them ?

Lots of country have demomakers like Finland, France, Usa, Canada, Japan, Israel etc...

The best way to meet demomakers is to go to Parties who are done especially for that. During 2 or 3 days, demomakers meet themself in a big place with big screen and sound system to compete.

Here are some parties links : The party, Assembly, Mekka & Symposium, Gathering, LTP.

Why demo arefree ?

As i say in 'what is a demomaker ?', they work only for fun. It's like war against other groups. They want to prove that they are best ones :)
