 'SIDsyn' SID synthesizer [08 Jun 2000]
Commodore Wire has informations about an upcoming new SID based synthesizer: "The synthesizer - named SIDsyn is built around the famous SID chip we all know and love. In true analogue tradition the device offers knobs for controlling the ADSR envelope, the filter’s resonance and cutoff. In addition there are switches for selecting the wave-forms and filter types (sqaure, sine, ..)". The SIDsyn will be cheaper than the SiDStation.
