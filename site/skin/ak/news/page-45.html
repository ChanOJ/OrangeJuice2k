 Innocent Sceners questioned [27 Feb 2000]
Last night a number of sceners visiting the Trax Party in Norway were questioned by the police. The reason they got detained was because they happened to be in a room at that time which was closed off of the party and the orginisers suspected these sceners were there for untrustworthy reasons. The truth however was that these sceners were just looking for some peace and quiet so they could talk and not be deathenned by the loud noises of the party hall. The sceners were eventually released.
