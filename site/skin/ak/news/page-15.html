 URARFileLib v0.3 [30 Aug 2000]
First public release of the free multi-OS (Win32, Linux and SunOS ) file library for the demoscene. The URARFileLib supports RAR archives (any compression method, including multimedia compression and encryption supported by RAR and WinRAR). It is easy to add to you project (1-2 lines of code) and tiny (5-8kB).
(Unique / Vantage)

 EuroLinux alliance Petition [23 Aug 2000]
To Xenon and the others: Hi I'm working in a huge law-firm and was confronted quite often with that problem. EU and national regulations provide sufficient protection for demos and similar creations (associated to art). The technical part can be industrial and thus should normally be "patentable". Regulations _have_ to change, we don't live in the 19th century anymore. More and more firms invest enormous summs into IT and should get the adequate protection. Or have you heard from any spoiled demomaker having "patent" troubles ??
(Madprocess / Psyko)
