Important decision to take. [26 May 2000]

Hi to all of you, sceners. Orange Juice exists for your pleasure for now
more than 3 years. Its birth happened on two french BBS and its goal was
always to serve you, using all of our limited means.
Today we've an important decision to take, and we want to ask you YOUR mind
about it.
We've added a vote in the charts to know if you agree or not with the idea
to put some advertising on the Orange Juice website. The money brought by
this advertising would be managed by our non-profit organisation ("Orange
Juice") and will be used to finance the next LTP (can you imagine an
almost-free non commercial party ? :).
Thanks to support us for this decision we've to take. Not to help ojuice
survive, but to go further in the great innovations Orange Juice showed to
you during the last years.

go to the vote

The Orange Juice team (Aevoc, Chandra, Yes)
