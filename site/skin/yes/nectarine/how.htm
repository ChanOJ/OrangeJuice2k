Instructions on how to start listening to the Nectarine music...

Step 1

Get a MP3 player that supports streaming (See below for a list).

 
Step 2

Open the URL ( http://????:8000 ) in your player or just click to ear the 24 kbps stream here.
(Choose "Open" when asked wheter you want to save or open the file)

 
Step 3

Request your favourite songs from the playlist. or from irc #nectarine on [ircnet] and do [!nectarine commands]

 
Step 4

Sit back and enjoy the music!
