Evoke & Radwar to cooperate

Evoke (www.evoke-net.de) and Radwar Enterprises (www.radwar.com) are
proud to announce their cooperation for the forthcoming demo-compo at
Evoke 2000.

Due to missing entries the demo-compo at Radwar 2000 got cancelled. As
the pricemoney of approx. 3000 EUR (6000 DM) was still available and had
to be given to the scene, the organizers of Evoke and Radwar decided to
cooperate.

Thus Radwar Enterprises are going to present the democompo at Evoke 2000
and donate the complete pricemoney to the winners of this compo.

In the spirit of Radwar this compo is open-platform now. Unlike other
compos at Evoke, entries on Amiga, Playstation, C64 and others will be
allowed. Rules of the demo-compo at Radwar do not apply.
