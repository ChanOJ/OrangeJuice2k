Who said that all demomakers are funny ? [06 Nov 2000]

It seems someone in the scene has taken a bit of a cheap shot at Jeff over at
Imphobia by going off and registering imphobia.com through www.namezero.com.

This nice site will bring up a porn pop-up window and link to "rawmen." View
the page source and we will see: Imphobia Diskmag - Proud to be gay! We now
make big $$$$ on you stupid demosceners. "imphobia diskmagazine scene
audiophonik dreams darkness jeff ivan gay " "Homepage of the shitty diskmag &
moneymakers of the demoscene."

Maybe the same people have registered "audiophonik.com" through namezero.com.
It is difficult to tell.

What I would like to understand is why the person who did this is pissed? Do
they understand this CD took over a year to create? The time Jeff, myself and
the others comitted? That all the musicians, and artist got paid cash and
CD's? That our intentions were to produce an outstanding CD that had
potential to sell well? That we have yet to sell a thousand CDs? That those
involved invested time and money to see the project through and not everyone
is making money on "audiophonik."

Ask any scene person who was around in the mid 90's and I think you'll find
that the Imphobia diskmag was the best diskmag being produced at the time.
Jeff has put a lot of time and effort into creating an excellent diskmag and
scene CD's for you and the rest of the scene. So, maybe a few dollars are
made on these CD's but keep in mind the time, effort, and investment usually
put the payment at less than what one would make at a minimum wage job.

I hope you can explain your grudge against Jeff and of course "audiophonik"
which I also spent time/effort/money on helping create.
