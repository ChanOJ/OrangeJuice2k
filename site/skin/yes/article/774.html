upload it to ftp://ftp.chscene.ch/incoming/bnz-jingles/ and win!

Theme: invitation-jingle for Buenzli #9
Format: MP3 - must be in a zip-file with description
Description: Name of composer, name of jingle
Size: it doesn't matter
Duration: max. 20 seconds

Upload your jingle to ftp://ftp.chscene.ch/incoming/bnz-jingles/ and
write a Mail with the filename and the description to compo@????,
