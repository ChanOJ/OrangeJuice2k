Trax in Space - Constatation [19 Sep 2000]

Trax In Space - Are they interested in demo scene musiciens ?

For some times, we are receiving more and more
"Ask for Help" dealing with internet site about demo scene.

Just after Scene.org that was asking for hardware and for
money to increase the available disc space, this is now Trax
In Space's turn to send a call for sceners. If the first call can
be understood because scene.org doesn't have any source
of earning, the Trax In Space's case is not so evident.

I've talk with several musicians, asking for their own
opinion about that.

At the beginning, the Trax In Space's Team has tried to
create a database for trackers musicians (xm, mod, s3m, it,
etc...) to help them to share their music. All it was
totally free.

50% of their users was musician coming from the scene,
the other part were casual musicians, professional ones,
and a little bit of lamers-rippers like everywhere.

But things have changed : TiS sold T-Shirt, cap,
CDs made from songs submited by the musicians during
the compos. That would have clean the financial disorder
according to the price of the articles : $12 for a cap...
Then TiS have began to sell artists CDs. That would have been
a good idea if they don't own 50% of the CDs price (TN : like the
very commercial site MP3.com). The 50% left are put in a
virtual purse you can use to buy TiS articles (with a new
prelevement from TiS I guess). If the artists want a check then
TiS took $5 in the pot.

° Note: Earnings reflected here can be used in the Express Shop when placing
° orders. Also checks are issued when a minimum amount of $50 has been reached; under no
° circumstances will cash be sent. There is a $5 processing fee assessed every time a check is
° sent to you.

° We collect a discount rate of 50% for each sale of a custom CD. You will
° receive 50% of the Net Selling Price (after discounts and promotions), and
° commissions made from our referral program will be deducted from your
° earnings. Your first sale is considered as a processing fee and,
° therefore, you will not receive credit for it.

Your first sell is not for you, all the money remain to TiS. The money
you earn is not the one that is printed on the internet site. How do
they fix the discount or promotion price ?

° ** How much do I make?
° Artists take 50% of their selling price (the minimum selling price is
° $7.99). Checks are mailed after a minimum sales amount has been reached.
° Furthermore the first CD sold goes toward paying for set-up costs. After
° the intial fee, the artist will receive 50% of the net selling price
° (after discounts and promotions), not 50% of the price listed.

And now, for the end, the VIP mode

If you want Trax In Space to review your brand new song you will need
to... pay. Of course everyone can review your song but VIP MEMBERS (how
they choose them) will review it if you pay your fee.

° Reviewing Priority -- We have hand-picked a few of the best reviewers on
° Trax In Space; and if you are a VIP member, they will provide your music
° with high-quality reviews that are great for feedback and promotion.

If you want to have an access to the samples database, you will also
have to pay.

° Reviewing Priority -- We have hand-picked a few of the best reviewers on
° Trax In Space; and if you are a VIP member, they will provide your music
° with high-quality reviews that are great for feedback and promotion.

All this samples must be free of charge. I hope that they're not samples
from artists' songs.

As you can see, you'll have to pay for all the services and TiS is now a
true commercial company with a sell department and so on. I don't think that
to help an internet site like this one is a good idea. If you really
want to help one, choose scene.org to help them to increase their
disk space and this is REALLY a site for the sceners.

Note From the Author : This article is only a constatation of what
TiS was and is becoming. If you use TiS and are satisfied of the
services they provide, just help them...
