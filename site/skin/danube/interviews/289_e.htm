Ecstasy interview by
Doctor Yes

English traduction : Deepmind
thanks to Ecstasy

OJ> Hello Ecstasy, could you introduce your group and yourself ?
E> Hello Doc, my name is Stephane Allegro, my non-scene nick is Clint, and I'm 23,75 :) NomaD is my main group. It had been founded by Z, whom we've had cool relationship when Pulpe was active ! (By the way, I wanna greet my dear Pulpe-men !)

OJ> Why did you enter the demoscene?
E> huhu, the first demo I've seen was Unreal. Only 1 year later I discovered Second Reality ! At that point I thought: "wow, I must create demos..." So I started alone, immediately starting to code a 3D engine. Then I discovered Internet and "hop!", TwoFace introduced me to aXs, and so I joined Pulpe. And here I am :) But at the beginning, the thing which gave me the will for coding was the game Stunt Machine on my CPC6128 :)

OJ> Immediately starting by 3D? Gasp! What do you just think about the current massive (and without design) use of 3D?
E> Yes, but I did some 3D stuff at the very beginning because I gasped in admiration watching this game... But I quickly understood I should had a break, and so I did, coding more conventional 2D effects... Personally I like it when it's well done and very clean, with lots of nice colours (like Doomsday or TBL) but It's plain that the demos I've been the most impressed by were Claudia and Genocyd and I try to make NomaD release demos with *scenarii* !! (did you hear that, NomaDs ? ;))

OJ> What do you like in the demoscene ?
 E> All :) music, pictures, the people, the competition, making the people been in Ecstasies over their computer (That's the reason of my nick, by the way), etc.

OJ> What do you think about guys who say the demoscene is already dead, or it should be soon?
E> huhu, people have been saying that for one year ! and we're still there... Those guys have no interest in the demoscene. I don't think the demoscene is dying ! There is so many things to be done with windows and 3DFX cards ;)) Errr... I shouldn't talk about windows: I may have ennemies now ;) DOS is dead not the demoscene.

OJ> Many groups think windows is not "demo compliant" ! What's your oppinion?
E> They are wrong. People are OK concerning Linux (why not?) but not for windows. Moreover, everybody has it (as everybody had DOS), and it's as performant as DOS for realtime calculating/animating since hardware is managed better.

OJ> Have you any planned windows releases?
E> With NomaD ?

OJ> Yes, or another group ;-)
E> Sure we have! There's a win32 version of Squeezed as for each of our future prods. Next year, we'll do only win32 prods, na ! :)

OJ> Did you came at Wired ?
E> I cannot commit myself. Either I'll take my holidays this Summer, or I'll go to the WiReD ! it depends of my job.

OJ> Bon, maintenant place aux traditionnels greetings.
E> greets come to axs, katana, redbug, magicfred, nytrik, SMK, DrYes, frigo, skal, roudoudou, made, skal, patapom, and all I've forgotten (LTP2,#demofr, etc, etc, etc.). and to NomaD, of course !

OJ> Thanks a lot! See you soon on our screens :)
