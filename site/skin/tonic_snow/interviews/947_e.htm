Statix interview by Chandra

OJ> Hello Statix !
S> Hi Chandra... and Orange Juice readers.

OJ> Please, present yourself...
S> I am 19, I am studying maths at the university of Cambridge in England, I have bright and crazy red hair (as Wired 96 veterans will know) and I am 6 feet tall (what's that in metres ? Hmm !). I also play a lot of classical music and play the cello...

OJ> How did you begin to code ?
S> I got a copy of Turbo Pascal 4 of an old guy who lived in my street... gradually the amount of assembler increased and soon I was writing stuff in full Turbo Assembler.

OJ> How did you hear about the demo scene ? When did you begin to code for demos ?
S> I have always been keen on sound, I heard mod files long before I knew about the demo scene. So I started writing trackers, since I had no net connection and needed a way to make these things.... then a friend showed me Panic and 2nd Reality and the rest, as they say, is history.

OJ> Are you in Psychic Links since its creation ? Psychic Links is no more active nowadays, isn't it ?
S> [nb editor - it's psychic link :) the French always add an s, I think I regret not having it originally !] Yeah, a friend from school had the idea for the name and another friend joined up as a coder. Our first demo, Act1, never got very far and was not wonderful (but it got me a job...) and it was only Juice in summer 95 (nb: 2nd at asm95) that got us some recognition on the scene... S-Cubed (the other coder) now works for some small games company, and Skywalker (the musician) has become a DJ... so I am the only person left. I release under PL, Acme and Imphobia now...

OJ> What was your first computer ?
S> Spectrum ! Hurray ! but I really started programming life on an Amstrad PC 1512, and stayed with my trusty 386 right up to assembly 95. Then I got a P90 which I still have. Boy do I need an upgrade !

OJ> Everybody has heard a lot of you recently (Paper, 303, Wired Intro 2). Apparently your style represent an average of the scene tastes. What do you think of this and how do you feel the future of the demoscene ?
S> I never heard that before ! But I still feel quite new to the scene as I have only had net access for 1 year now... I think my style in my head is quite consistent and what I want, perhaps I am just getting better at producing it. I think the scene often goes too far up its own arse.... There are a lot of very boring "design" demos out, getting almost as bad as all those vector shows of 1 or 2 years ago. That's not to say there isn't some great stuff and some beautiful new effects/fashions but still most demos that I have downloaded recently have been disappointingly all the same. The scene seems to have ups and downs of creativity (excluding some individuals, who I worship :-) and won't name here) and at the moment we are on a low, but increasing.

OJ> Do you think there is too many coding parties ? Near 60th a year...
S> I think that you can't have too many parties ! Without parties the scene dies and becomes a very solitary sport. Parties are not just competitions, they bring everyone together to have a nice time.

OJ> What are your programming tools ?
S> These have changed a lot, I used to write 100% asm. I use my tracker and player in 100% asm, using Microsoft's assembler, it's much nicer, and Paper was written like that... for bigger stuff I now use Watcom C and Assembler mixed... Although I wish I could use Microsoft Visual C++ 5, which is really cool ! (if bugged...). I also use Pascal sometimes for fast tool making... And let's not forget Pascal's Indoor Music System which I first used for the Wired invitation and which is great. Pascal is a great coder.

OJ> How do you work ? A lot in a little time, or a lot of time for a little work ?
S> I binge. That is, I think a lot about demos but do nothing. I often have great long visions of how something should be, or what I want, and do nothing. then I will do a week of coding every night after my studies... and then nothing... for 303, I worked very gradually on all the mpeg and sound stuff, and then suddenly I had to work really head when I went to stay with Vic and at The Party. I never want to do that again ! I have never worked that hard for so long... (3 solid 24 hour days). The results were unfortunately very rough as a result - but I hope to make a new demo with Vic some day that will be what 303 should have been....

OJ> What are the things you don't like in 303 ?
S> Well there are many rough edges in the release version - particularly palette fuckups and MANY effects I wanted to try and didn't make it in. I had a great vision for it... but it wasn't 303 :) next time... I hope to spend a lot more time smoothing it :)

OJ> What did your non-scene friends think about your scene activities ?
S> My parents really like it, but don't understand it :-) my girlfriend hates it and all things related to it, and just smiles quietly whenever I mention demos.... and all my friends at uni think it's quite amusing 1 time but then ignore it later. It's the same all over the world isn't it? isn't it ?

OJ> Would you like to work at Microsoft ?
S> No, not enough freedom there. Simple.

OJ> What kind of music do you like ?
S> Hmm, difficult one that one! Classical first : anything Russian, esp. shostakovitch, and esp. string stuff. Otherwise, here are the albums I most recently listened to: portishead/portishead, sneaker pimps, lamb (superb !), dubstar (both albums), prodigy (hmm... don't like it so much any more...), olive (superb again !) oh hell I talk to much :-)

OJ> Thank you Statix, do you want to say a last word ?
S> Thanks for the interest. I hope loads of people get into the demoscene who can bring some new life in. Sometimes I think it gets a bit cliched.... but hell lets have fun !

S> Jeez that sounded shite. Ok I'm tired ok ? Cheese comes from tired people. Just be happy.

S> Ok so now it's a night later and I am not so tired... but I still think the same thing :) sometimes the scene goes too far up its own arse (me included) so hell let's just have fun. the finns certainly showed it at asm95 ... too bad I haven't visted there for so long !
